Story

Our story

Two worlds,
one grill

Fusion Fast opened on Brixton Road in the summer of 2026 with one idea: take the Lebanese grill we love and push it somewhere new with Asian spice. Here's how a plate comes together.

01

Marinate

Chicken, lamb and wings sit in our house marinade before they go anywhere near the heat. That's where the flavour starts, and it's the bit we never rush.

02

Charcoal

Real charcoal, real flame. Skewers, kofta and wings go over the coals so they come off charred on the outside and juicy in the middle.

03

The spit

Our doner and shawarma turn slowly on the spit and get carved when you order, so every wrap is fresh off the stack.

04

The fusion

This is where we go our own way. Naga for heat, shatkora for that sharp citrus kick, and a spicy rice that holds its own next to anything on the grill.

05

The box

Everything lands in a box, hot, and goes straight over the counter. Take it home, take it to a mate's, or eat it on the walk back.
