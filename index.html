    Good to know

    Questions,
    answered

    Is Fusion Fast halal?

    Yes, our food is halal. If you have a question about a particular dish, just ask us when you order and we will talk you through it.

    Where is Fusion Fast?

    We are at 284 Brixton Road, London SW9 6AG, on the main road through Brixton. Stockwell, Kennington and Camberwell are all a short ride away.

    What time are you open?

    We open at 2pm every day and stay open late into the night. If you are coming after midnight, give us a quick call on 020 8075 1647 first.

    Can I order for collection?

    Yes. Build your order on our Order page and send it to us on WhatsApp, or call 020 8075 1647. You pay when you pick it up, with no app fees on top.

    Do you deliver?

    Yes, you can get us delivered through Uber Eats and Just Eat. If you live nearby, collecting direct from us saves you the app charges.

    Do you serve alcohol?

    No. We only serve soft drinks, so it is a relaxed spot for everyone.

    What should I order on my first visit?

    Go for the Mix Grill if you are hungry, the Fusion Naga Doner if you like heat, or the Fusion Fast Box if you cannot decide. It has a bit of everything.

    Do you have vegetarian options?

    Yes. Falafel, grilled halloumi, hummus and our falafel and halloumi wraps are all meat free. Let us know about any allergies when you order.
